Our Features

Everything your little one needs to learn, play and grow in a caring Indian preschool and daycare environment.

Preschool & Daycare

NEP 2020-aligned play-based programs for Nursery, LKG, UKG & full-day daycare.

NTT-Trained Teachers

Caring Montessori & Early Childhood educators who give every child personal attention.

Safe & Child-Friendly Campus

CCTV-monitored, hygienic classrooms with child-safe furniture and secure entrances.

Healthy Indian Meals

Nutritious vegetarian thali, wholesome snacks and a restful afternoon nap time.

Safe Pickup & Drop

GPS-tracked school vans with trained attendants for complete peace of mind.

Fun Activities & Festivals

Dance, music, art and celebration of Indian festivals like Diwali, Holi & Pongal.

Our Activities

Thoughtfully planned activities help children explore, create and grow through joyful, age-appropriate experiences every day.

  • Music
  • Learn
  • Painting
  • Sports
  • Playground

Music

Songs, rhythm and movement make every day joyful while building young children's listening, language and coordination skills.

Children enjoy action songs, Indian rhymes, simple instruments and festive music in English and familiar regional languages. These playful sessions encourage confidence, expression and participation.

  • Indian Rhymes
  • Action Songs
  • Rhythm & Movement
  • Simple Instruments
  • Language Skills
  • Confident Expression
View Details

Learn

Play-based learning builds strong foundations in language, early mathematics, curiosity and everyday independence.

Stories, phonics, picture talk, puzzles and hands-on activities introduce letters, numbers, shapes and our surroundings. Each child learns at a comfortable pace with caring teacher guidance.

  • Early Phonics
  • Number Readiness
  • Storytelling
  • Hands-on Discovery
  • Thinking Skills
  • School Readiness
View Details

Painting

Colourful art experiences give little hands the freedom to imagine, experiment and proudly create something of their own.

Children explore crayons, finger painting, clay, collage and safe craft materials. Seasonal and Indian festival-themed projects make creativity meaningful while strengthening fine-motor control.

  • Drawing & Colouring
  • Clay Modelling
  • Festival Crafts
  • Creative Expression
  • Fine-Motor Skills
  • Sensory Exploration
View Details

Sports

Age-appropriate games keep children active and help develop balance, coordination, teamwork and healthy daily habits.

Through mini races, ball games, yoga, movement drills and fun team challenges, children strengthen their bodies while learning patience, fair play and the joy of taking part.

  • Fun Fitness
  • Kids' Yoga
  • Ball Games
  • Balance & Coordination
  • Team Spirit
  • Healthy Habits
View Details

Playground

Safe, supervised outdoor play gives children space to move freely, make friends and discover the world around them.

Climbing, sliding, sand play and group games build strength, confidence and social skills. Teachers encourage sharing, cooperation and independent play in a caring environment.

  • Supervised Play
  • Safe Equipment
  • Sand & Nature Play
  • Social Confidence
  • Sharing & Teamwork
  • Gross-Motor Skills
View Details
